### What is CDQ Fraud Guard, and what are its main features?

Answer:

> CDQ Fraud Guard is a service designed to help businesses protect themselves from payment fraud and ensure secure, compliant financial transactions by verifying bank account information and monitoring for fraudulent activity. It operates through a community-driven database of validated bank accounts and known fraud cases to maintain transaction integrity.
>
> **Main Features**:
>
> 1. **Bank Account Verification**: Checks new bank account details against a shared database of validated accounts before use.
> 2. **Customizable Trust Score**: Assigns risk ratings based on transaction history, adjustable to match a business’s specific fraud tolerance.
> 3. **Real-Time Fraud Alerts**: Notifies users of potential fraud attacks using community-shared fraud case data.
> 4. **Fraud Case Management**: Allows businesses to document, track, and resolve fraud incidents efficiently.
> 5. **Seamless Integration**: Provides an API for easy connection with existing financial systems without requiring custom interfaces.
>
> The service emphasizes enhanced security, operational efficiency, and community collaboration to reduce financial risks while maintaining compliance.
